About the Project

The NBT Library is the successor to The Shulker Archives. Focused on a "quality over quantity" approach, it provides meticulously curated hotbars full of items, kits, books, and more for your creative servers and single-player worlds.

Categories

Utility & Abuse

Kits and Items made to make life easier or harder for yourself and other players.

PvP

Fully enchanted armor and weapons, for all forms of combat.

Potions

Custom brewed potions with extended effects and levels.

Fireworks & Spawn Eggs

Fireworks with custom explosions, and various unique spawn eggs.

Misc. Functional

Items that have functionality, but don't fit into any other category.

Lore & Non-Functional

Unique items with custom lore, but little or no functional purpose.

Books

Player-written books, including Fiction, Non-Fiction, Command, and Abuse.

SongPlayer

Items made for the SongPlayer mod, with song data encoded in them.

MarioKartWii & Txsla

Kits and Items made by MarioKartWii and Txsla, two of the greatest item-makers.

Disclaimer

  • Version Specific: Releases are strictly tied to specific Minecraft Java Edition versions and will not work on others.
  • Creative Only: Files are intended strictly for Creative mode usage in single-player or multiplayer worlds.
  • No Survival/Bedrock: Survival mode and Minecraft Bedrock Edition are not supported.
  • Use at Your Own Risk: Some items can cause lag, crashes, or corruption. The author is not responsible for damaged worlds or servers.
